What's new

V1.0.0 - Oct 2026

  • Portfolio landing page.
  • Menubar shows links to Portfolio home, a modal for Caregiver Tracker where you can signup/login.
  • Caregiver Tracker:
    • First time users must choose who to take care.
    • Care Taken Detail at the right end of the page shows card of person you are taking care of.
    • You can switch between persons you take care of.
    • Feeding can be tracked and it can be edited or deleted.
    • Excretion, Medication can be logged and it cannot be edited or deleted now.
    • Inventory can be added but cannot be edited or deleted.
    • Inventory must be added for Medication and some forms of Excretion logging.
    • Logout redirects back to Portfolio home.
